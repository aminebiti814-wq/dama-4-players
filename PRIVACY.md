# Privacy Policy for Dama Royale

**Last updated: 29 October 2026**

Dama Royale is a board game app published by Amine Biti.

## What this app collects

**Nothing.** Dama Royale does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal
information about you.

The app has no sign-in, no accounts and no user profiles. It asks for no name,
no email address, no phone number and no location. It contains no advertising
and no analytics or tracking software of any kind.

## Internet access

Dama Royale has no permission to access the internet. The whole game, including
the computer opponents, runs on your own device. It works with the phone in
airplane mode, and it cannot send data anywhere even if it wanted to.

## What is stored on your device

The app remembers two small settings so the game opens the way you left it:

- Which players are set to human or computer, the chosen game mode, and the
  computer difficulty
- Whether sound is switched on

These settings are saved only inside the app on your own phone. They are never
uploaded, never shared, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Permissions

The app requests a single Android permission, **Vibrate**, which is used to make
the phone buzz briefly when a piece is captured. It is used for nothing else.

## Children

Dama Royale is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data at all,
it collects no data from children either.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with
a new date at the top.
